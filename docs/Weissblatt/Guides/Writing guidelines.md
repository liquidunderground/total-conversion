# Article Naming

Articles should be named uniquely and easy to remember. When the subject has multiple names or aliases, choose the most general, objective and lore-oriented name and add non-redundant aliases to your article's [[YAML Properties|front matter]] (example: [[Flying Bazaar|Hurricane Souq Zone]] as an alias to [[Flying Bazaar]]).

When referring to a heavily-aliased subject within another article, use the most appropriate alias in context (e.g. "[[Alex Felitas]] encountered [[Fishbart Toyfeller|Dr. Roy Asura]] near the [[Enlightenment Crystal]]").

Should there be two unrelated subjects sharing the same name, use parentheses to distinguish them (e.g. `Planet Weissblatt (Planet)` and `Planet Weissblatt (Website)`).

# Sections and Structure

Do not create subsections without reason. Here's a few useful rules-of-thumb:

1. Don't waste the reader's time.
2. Start each article with an introductory paragraph summarizing the subject as simply as possible.
3. Each (Sub)section should start with at least one meaningful paragraph of text.
4. Only create subsections when you have at least two distinct things to talk about.

Try not to split your subject across multiple articles when possible. For example, use subsections to structure [[Stella Mountain]]'s appearances across the #Delfid, #Yuko and #Zweitausend canons respectively.
