The *seven scrolls of Neumos* are magical texts which are said to have enabled [[Primon]] to found the [[Marble Empire]] and become it's ideal ruler, before passing them and the empire onto [[Cleron]].

# History
(Full article: [[Yuko Wars]])

Although the existence of - let alone the exact origins of the Scrolls of Neumos are unknown, legend has it that these scrolls allowed [[Primon]] to have ascended to the ideal ruler and found the [[Marble Empire]].

Primon's son [[Cleron]] was believed to know about the whereabouts and contents of the scrolls due to his relation to Primon, but none of this could be confirmed before the siege on the imperial palace by [[Shogun Yuko]]'s troops, where Yuko ceremoniously tied a scroll to a rocket and destroyed it, claiming it to be one of the scrolls of Neumos.

Throughout the years counterfeits have also emerged on various black markets, most of them of poor quality due to the originals's contents being largely unknown. In contrast to these, another myth emerged that the true meaning can be deciphered and their powers unleashed only from the originals directly.
