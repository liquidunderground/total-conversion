*Elise Cantor* is an AI swan-stork-mix musician and one of the main playable characters of [[The Asura Incident]].

Elise was originally built by a young [[Kleene Cognara]], but eventually became autonomous. She developed a fascination for music and performs as "ELI5E", an idol spcializing in Jazz and electronica.

Her body is little more than an illusion, being projected out from her helmet with just enough solidity to serve as a means of transport. Accordingly, she mostly speaks from the speakers on the side of her helmet and could potentially change appearance at will, although she prefers her current bird form.

Since her helmet lacks persistent storage, she may occasionally lay "Zip Eggs" containing a snapshot of her AI. This process is extremely exhausting to Elise, usually requiring a reboot afterwards using the freshly-laid egg.
