*Dua* is a fugitive phoenix-kitsune hybrid from the [[Yuko Wars]] era. After her home town got pillaged by an official of the [[Marble Empire]] under [[Shogun Yuko]], she set out to find the seven [[Scrolls of Neumos]] and found [[Byodo]].
