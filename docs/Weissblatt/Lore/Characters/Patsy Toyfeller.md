*Patsy Toyfeller* is an unlockable character from [[The Asura Incident]]. He is the co-founder of [[Toyfeller Corp]] and younger brother of [[Fishbart Toyfeller]].
# History

Already at young age, Patsy loved entertaining others with silly antics, for which he'd often use self-built props built from toy's he'd take apart and reassemble in his own way. This led him to devise his own system of engineering from self-studying the toys that came across his way.

Between 1974 and 1977 [[Planet Weissblatt#Weissblatt Calendar|WBC]] Patsy tried to enroll in university to study engineering in order to pursue a career in toymaking, but to no avail. Although his constructs were impressive, his methods of creating them were considered so antithetical and alien to all common engineering practise that he was universally rejected. Left otherwise locked out of his dream career by 1977 WBC, he decided to trust the business plans proposed by his brother [[Fishbart Toyfeller]] and co-founded [[Toyfeller Corp]].

After initial success Patsy initially objected to the idea of mass-producing his toy designs at [[Toyfeller Corp|Toyfeller Park]]. Recognizing the necessity to keep the brothers afloat however, he eventually complied. From 1984 [[Planet Weissblatt#Weissblatt Calendar|WBC]] onward Patsy grew burnt-out from the lack of cretive freedom and the increasing demands placed upon him, leading to quality control issues within Toyfeller Corp's production.

Between 1992 and 1997 [[Planet Weissblatt#Weissblatt Calendar|WBC]] Patsy worked on the infrastructure of Toyfeller Park as per request of Fishbart, although his mental state deteriorated even further. During this time, Patsy was also regularly visited by a demon during moments of solitude, offering to grant him a wish through a contract.
In 1997 [[Planet Weissblatt#Weissblatt Calendar|WBC]], Patsy had a mental breakdown and suddenly left Toyfeller Corp without notice.

In 1999 [[Planet Weissblatt#Weissblatt Calendar|WBC]], the planet-wide scramble for the [[Enlightenment Crystal#Chakra Shards|Chakra Shards]] led to Patsy being discovered by [[Volunteers]], living and performing in a giant self-built desert circus within the [[Unnamed Desert]]. This encounter was soon interjected by [[Shtayn|MARA]] stealing the demonic contract to Patsy's soul.

After recovery following his soul being retrieved from [[The Underworld]], Patsy did not return back to Toyfeller Corp.
