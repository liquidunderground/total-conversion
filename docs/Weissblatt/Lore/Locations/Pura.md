*Pura* is one of the six moons of [[Planet Weissblatt]]. It is said to symbolize purity and discipline.

# Locations
## White steppe
During [[The Asura Incident]], the white steppe served as a special stage set on Pura. Completing it would yield one of the [[Enlightenment Crystal#Chakra Shards]].
