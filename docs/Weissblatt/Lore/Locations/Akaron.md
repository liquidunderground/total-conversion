*Akaron* is one of the six moons of [[Planet Weissblatt]]. It is said to symbolize focus and stability.

# Locations
## Piantonic grove
During [[The Asura Incident]], the piantonic grove served as a jungle-themed special stage set on Akaron. Completing it would yield one of the [[Enlightenment Crystal#Chakra Shards]].

# Notes
- The name *Akaron* is based on the indonesian word "akar", meaning focus.
