*Byodo* is the state founded by [[Dua]] in between the #Delfid and #Yuko eras. It's capital *(Neon) Centropolis* serves as a bonus stage in [[The Asura Incident]].

# History

Byodo was founded by [[Dua]] sometime after the [[Yuko Wars]] with the ambition of being a republic without discrimination of any kind.

After Dua's death however, her life's work has been mythologized by her successors for propagandistic purposes and political corruption has led Byodo to become a totalitarian surveillance state. After various power struggles throughout this process, Byodo's political leadership decried [[Delfid]]'s use of crystal-based magic as mysticism in favor of technology that can be rigorously studied and controlled. As such Byodo is generally highly advanced in traditional technology, which it uses to control it's citizens. The occasional artifact of Delfidian magic may be spotted in Byodo, although it's application is highly constrained.
