# Description

The *Flying Bazaar* is a loose congregation of sky pirates located within the eye of one of [[Planet Weissblatt]]'s tsunamis. It is inspired by [Barbary Pirates], [Skies of Arcadia], *Windy Valley* from [Sonic Adventure] and [The Cathedral and the Bazaar] [The Egg Carrier]. During [[The Asura Incident]], it is referred to as *Hurricane Souq*.

# History

Following the initial destruction of [[Delfid]] during [[The Asura Incident]], [[Alex Felitas]] is left stranded on the Flying Bazaar without magical powers and is quickly captured and enslaved, until they are found rescued by volunteers.

Following the scramble for [[Frozen Record Zone]] at the time, Delfid's previously-classified magical knowledge was leaked through the bazaar by means of black market trading. The following arms race for magic and the [[Enlightenment Crystal#Chakra Shards]] caused a loose yet strong force of rebel pirates to form against [[Byodo]] and the [[Nautical League]] during [[Zweitausend]].
# Trivia

- The term "Souq" from *Hurricane Souq Zone* stems from arabic and (much like the term bazaar), refers to a type of market.
