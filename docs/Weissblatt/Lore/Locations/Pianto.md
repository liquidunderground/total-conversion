*Pianto* is one of the six moons of [[Planet Weissblatt]]. It is said to symbolize life and patience.

# Locations
## Piantonic grove
During [[The Asura Incident]], the piantonic grove served as a jungle-themed special stage set on Pianto. Completing it would yield one of the [[Enlightenment Crystal#Chakra Shards]].
