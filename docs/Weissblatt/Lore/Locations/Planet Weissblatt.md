# Moons

Planet Weissblatt features six moons, each associated with their own mythological symbolism:
- [[Aura]] represents wealth and generosity
- [[Akaron]] represents focus and stability
- [[Nautri]] represents wisdom and the sea
- [[Pianto]] represents patience and nature
- [[Pura]] represents purity and discipline
- [[Tauron]] represents power and endurance

# Weissblatt Calendar
The *Weissblatt Calendar* or *WBC* is planet Weissblatt's domestic calendar. It's mainly a means to keep Weissblatt's timeline ordered within this Wiki as a teaching tool.

Each year is denoted as a number followed by the initials "WBC". Dates before Weissblatt's [Epoch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epoch) 0 WBC should be denoted using negative numbers, e.g "-123 WBC" (although there's currently no canonical reason to do so).

As Weissblatt is a made-up fantasy planet, years, months, days, etc. do not correspond to the [Gregorian calendar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gregorian_calendar) and cannot be mapped to the sequences of real-life history.
