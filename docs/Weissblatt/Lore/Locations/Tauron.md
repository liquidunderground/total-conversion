*Tauron* is one of the six moons of [[Planet Weissblatt]]. It is said to symbolize focus and stability.

# Locations
## Tauran Arena
During [[The Asura Incident]], the piantonic grove served as a colloseum set inside of a moon crater of Tauron. As a special stage, completing it would yield one of the [[Enlightenment Crystal#Chakra Shards]].
