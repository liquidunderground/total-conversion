*Aura* is one of the six moons of [[Planet Weissblatt]]. It is said to symbolize wealth and generosity.

# Locations
## Auran Casino
During [[The Asura Incident]], the auran casino served as a casino-themed special stage set on Aura. Completing it would yield one of the [[Enlightenment Crystal#Chakra Shards]].

# Notes
- The name "Aura" is a reference to the latin word for gold.
