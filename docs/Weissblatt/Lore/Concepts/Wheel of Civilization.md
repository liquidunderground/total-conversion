The *Wheel of Civilization* is a philosophical concept originating from the [[Marble Empire]]. It describes how social forces, particularly those of *decadence*, *tyranny* and *chaos* can cause political unrest and perpetuate an eternal cycle of social change.

# Theory

According to the theory, the progression of civilization is spun in a metaphocal wheel, moved across phases by the forces of *decadence*, *tyranny* and *chaos* influencing one another.

1. Decadence is exploitative, inciting unrest from the underclass.
2. Tyranny is oppressive and controlled, leading into chaos
3. Chaos is uncontrolled and messy.

According to the theory, this cycle can potentially be stopped or broken. Whether stopping the wheel of civilization is aspirational or devastating to society however is unknown.
# Trivia
- The Wheel of Civilization was inspired by [social cycle theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_cycle_theory), particularly that of [Polybius](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polybius) As well as the buddhist [Bhavacakra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bhavacakra)
