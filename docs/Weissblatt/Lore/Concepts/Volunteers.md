*Volunteers* are an affiliation during [[The Asura Incident]]. The term describes characters which decided to volunteer in collecting the [[Enlightenment Crystal#Chakra Shards]]  in accordance with the distress signal broadcast by [[Alex Felitas]], regardless of their affiliation with [[Delfid]].

# Characters
Although any add-on character for [[The Asura Incident]] may potentially be a volunteer, the default cast features the following volunteer characters:

- [[Kleene Cognara]]
- [[Elise Cantor]]
- [[Babel Memto]]
- [[Alex Felitas]] (through their affiliation w/ [[Delfid]])
