# Weissblatt Zweitausend - The Next Millennium

## Lore/Backstory

After the king of the underworld had been defeated, the underworld, the [[Crystal Clockworld]] and [[Planet Weissblatt]] remained interconnected. Left stranded across the planet without a ruler, demons started possessing the planet's population to integrate into regular society. The line between animate and inanimate - demonic and pure - blurred.

Once previously classified magic of the Delfid clergy made it's way onto the [[Flying Bazaar]], magical literacy soon turned into an arms race for magical technology and the race for the Chakra Shards continued.

By the mark of the next millennium, the planet was divided into three factions, stuck in a stalemate over crystals and control over the six lunar colonies:


**The Byodan Empire**
: A totalitarian empire controlling 45% of the planet's landmasses as well as the moons Tauron and Akaron. They date their history back over 2000 years to the days of the Yuko wars and have since exploited the old Delfid knowledge of magic for technological and military gain.

**The Nautical League**
: A defense bloc against the increasing encroachment of the Byodan Empire initiated by the Bouwder and the former Delfid clergy. They make up 35% and control the moons Blanche and Pianto. They seek control of the Chakra Shards and the lunar colonies to reassemble the Enlightenment Crystal and build a second Delfid.

**The Outlaws**
: A decentralized underclass of pirates, terrorists, diasporic demons, and other independent actors who managed to resist. These make up the remaining 20% of land as well as the moons Aura and Nautri.


## Themes

- Generational Decay (Byodo; Nautical League)
- Learning from the past vs. improving the future (Nautical empire vs. Byodo vs. Outlaws)
- Exploitation (War over lunar colonies; militaristic crystal hunt)
- Collective vs. personal morality (Outlaws)
- Loss of control (Byodo, Alex)
